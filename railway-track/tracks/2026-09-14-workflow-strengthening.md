- Offline serialization does not prove the provider will accept every future request or return
  useful reasoning summaries. Unsupported settings fail visibly; no silent fallback is added.
- Native compaction thresholds remain 250K/100K. Forced fixtures prove retrievability, not real
  summary fidelity or optimal repeated single-message compaction. Inspect the new selected/retained
  records before tuning it. More tracing increases local storage use; protect it as research data.
- Cancellation during the synchronous domain phase waits for that worker to exit before releasing
  the workflow lock, then stops without creating research. Python cannot safely kill its thread.
  Native research cancellation/client cleanup is unchanged. Do not start a second writer.
- A separately authorized live comparison must evaluate source relevance, observed readability,
  retained qualifications and externally supported risks/opportunities/actions.
