# Product Vision

## Product Purpose

CDI Deep Research organizes supplied evidence around industry plugins and user requirements. The implemented Layer 2 scope is industry-neutral; existing Layers 3/4 remain real-estate research. The maintained product description remains in [README.md](../README.md).

## Target Users

Not established.

## Principles

Preserve evidence provenance, resumability, partial publication, and model-output freedom. The controlling repository rules remain in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Boundaries

Generated runs, secrets, caches, sources, and checkpoints remain local. Model-output and agent-security boundaries remain authoritative in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Major Capabilities

Layer 2 designs and reviews dynamic domains while preserving evidence and disclosing unresolved recorded facts. Layer 3 researches property risks, and Layer 4 researches external influences. Dynamic Layer 2 downstream integration remains separate; current behavior is documented in [README.md](../README.md).

## Long-Term Direction

Support different industry plugins and user priorities without hardcoded Layer 2 domain rosters. Integrating dynamic domains into downstream research is separate work, not part of the present implementation.
