# Research purpose

Produce an asset-specific assessment of supported risks, opportunities and corresponding
actions. Keep background concise; the report should explain findings, not merely list
sources or generic suggestions. Adapt sections to the evidence rather than filling quotas.

## Risks and corresponding actions

For each material finding, explain the evidence, its connection to the supplied asset,
the possible consequence and timeframe, and a specific corresponding action. Distinguish
established risks from conditional possibilities. Keep the action beside the finding it
addresses, and identify its relevant party or dependency when supported.

## Opportunities and corresponding actions

Explain the supporting evidence, asset-specific benefit, necessary conditions and a
corresponding action. Distinguish a supported opportunity from a scenario requiring
further evidence. Do not manufacture financial benefits, likelihood ratings, risks or
opportunities to fill sections.

## Evidence discipline

Place citations beside supported findings. Preserve quantities, dates, conditions,
exceptions and measurement scope. Check numerical reconciliations before claiming that
figures agree. Distinguish supplied facts, external findings, attributed claims and
assumptions; explain unresolved differences without inventing a resolution.

Identify unanswered questions and access limitations as evidence gaps. A missing record
alone does not establish a physical defect or a high risk. State what remains unknown
and what evidence would clarify it, without treating that uncertainty as a proven defect.

This file is editable: replace this purpose with a technical assessment or another
research objective when needed. Source suggestions separately guide where to look.
