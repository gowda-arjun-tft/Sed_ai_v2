Why the output is generic—and the final correction

The research machinery ran. The prompts aimed it at the wrong product: a broad due-diligence decision memo instead of current, property-linked risk intelligence.

Final issue: Layer 2 creates repeated domain mandates rather than property-specific risk questions. Layer 3 then researches the domain generally and is explicitly told to produce decisions, actions, scenarios and recommendations. The result is predictable: fact-sheet repetition, law and macro summaries, missed nearby developments, weak geopolitical discovery, and an advisory final report the user did not ask for.

Search volume was not the limiting factor. Search targeting and the final output contract were. The completed Bad Homburg run provides a clean baseline for the diagnosis.

How the generic output is produced

The failure is cumulative. Each stage faithfully amplifies the ambiguity supplied by the previous stage.

1

Layer 2 repeats mandates

Nine chunks each write another version of “establish everything.” Specific anomalies remain context, not research questions.

2

Lenses follow generic roles

Academic finds laws, economist finds macro data, historian finds precedents. None owns “this address, now, nearby.”

3

Search drifts outward

National frameworks and market context dominate. Current local construction, planning and events are not deliberately scanned.

4

Synthesis writes advice

The prompt asks for decision gates, actions, owners and investment guidance—so the final report becomes an underwriting memo.

Root causes by layer

Layer 2

The mission is a mandate, not a question

Each chunk creates another broad mission paragraph. The merge preserves all nine, so Layer 3 receives repeated instructions to investigate an entire domain. It does not receive a concise list of property anomalies, risk hypotheses, search anchors and open questions.

The mission also lacks a stable address/geo block containing the street, district, parcel and relevant neighboring sites.

Inspect the Layer 2 router prompt

Layer 3

The requested product is wrong

The coordinator is instructed to make a decision-ready report with consequences, scenarios, handoffs and actions. The synthesizer requests an investment decision, owners and decision gates. Generic advice is therefore compliance with the prompt, not model disobedience.

The shared rules classify evidence as established, inferred, unknown or immaterial, but do not require a property-risk verdict or causal linkage.

The five Layer 3 prompt defects

These are separate behavioral gaps. Fixing only the report format would still leave the research generic; fixing only search wording would still produce unwanted advice.

Defect 1

Lenses aim at disciplines

Academic, economic and historical roles naturally collect laws, statistics and precedents. Their prompts do not make the property the compulsory subject of every search and finding.

Defect 2

No environmental scan

No lens owns current local news, nearby construction, planning applications, transport works, council decisions, major events or announced projects.

Defect 3

No complete risk vocabulary

The shared rules can say established, inferred, unknown or immaterial, but they do not consistently require likelihood, severity, timing and a building-or-people effect.

Defect 4

Advice is explicitly ordered

Scenarios, handoffs, actions, owners and decision gates are requested. The unwanted underwriting memo is therefore the instructed output.

Defect 5

Geopolitics has no discovery checklist

A quiet fact sheet produces a quiet mission. No prompt translates geopolitics into tenant-state, energy, vendor, sanction, cyber, security and specialist-capacity pathways for this building.

Cross-cutting

No novelty rule

The reports are not told to focus on what public evidence adds, contradicts or updates. Repeating the supplied fact sheet is therefore allowed and often rewarded.

One mechanical defect also exists

The nine-fold mission duplication is caused by concatenating chunk missions without deduplication. It can be reduced through the router prompt by emitting only chunk-specific questions, but deterministic deduplication is the one issue that is not purely semantic. The broader genericness remains a prompt-objective problem.

What Layer 2 should hand to Layer 3

Keep the permanent domain mandate once. The generated mission should contain only the property-specific research delta: trigger, hypothesis, search anchors and missing linkage.

Current mission

“Establish everything”

Establish condition, compliance, capacity, inspection status, cost, timing, responsibility, disruption and residual risk—repeated with slightly different wording for every chunk.

Effect: broad domain research and repeated fact-sheet narration.

Required mission

Property-specific question

Property fact: employee and construction access may depend on municipal swimming-pool land.

Risk hypothesis: planned works, events, access-policy changes or third-party restrictions may interrupt staff, visitors, contractors or emergency access.

Search anchors: Seedammweg, Seedammbad, Stadtwerke, municipal plans, road closures, event calendar and access agreements.

Missing linkage: whether the arrangement is current, legally protected, temporary or revocable.

The target research behavior

Every material finding should prove how external evidence reaches the actual property. News and events are inputs, not risks by themselves.

New evidence→ Property fact→ Exposure→ Vulnerability→ Building / people effect→ Time horizon→ Risk verdict

Property fact
Staff, visitors and contractors rely on constrained local access and parking.
Possible effect
Temporary access disruption, noise, travel delay or operating interference.

Established event; property impact inferred until route and timing overlap are verified.
Not included
No recommendation, action owner, investment decision or generic transport commentary.

Two discovery areas that must become explicit

Nearby intelligence

Use tiered proximity, not one blind radius

Immediate: parcel, neighboring properties, access streets, parking and utilities.

Operational catchment: roadworks, transit changes, construction, noise, events, demonstrations, emergency access and service interruptions.

Municipality/region: planning, public projects, court/service changes, major employers and market developments only when they can reach the property.

Geopolitical transmission

Research pathways, not world news

Energy: supply or price change → building heat/power reliability and Opex.

Equipment: trade/vendor restriction → fire, lift, controls or electrical parts → repair delay and operational disruption.

Occupier/security: state budget, court reform, cyber or justice-site threat → occupancy, people or building operations.

Labour/materials: specialist scarcity or commodity disruption → works programme, downtime and cost exposure.

Required output contract

The reader must be able to see what was already known, what the web added, how the risk was inferred, and what remains unknown. Mixing those four categories creates repetition and false confidence.

Final synthesizer: merge duplicate findings that share the same causal chain, preserve distinct risks with different causes, show cross-domain amplification, separate current/emerging/long-term risks, retain sources and confidence, and report important “not established” results. What does not need redesign yet

Keep

STORM coordination

The fan-out, coordinator and verifier can support the desired product once their responsibilities are corrected.

Keep

Current search tools

Ground/Climate and Rights already demonstrate that address-specific German research is possible with the existing tooling.

Keep

Primary-source discipline

German-language queries, official sources, source reading and citation verification are strengths worth preserving.

Acceptance test for the next prompt iteration

  • Every domain performs property-anchored, time-aware research rather than generic domain research.
  • The U2 works and Steinkaut S-Bahn proposal are found and linked cautiously to the property.
  • The geopolitics domain tests concrete building, occupier, energy, vendor, cyber and security pathways.
  • No acquisition advice, action owner, decision gate, approval, rejection or repricing language appears.
  • Every reported risk contains evidence, property linkage, affected building/people outcome, timing and status.
  • A fact-sheet fact is repeated only when it is needed to explain a new contradiction, update or risk pathway.
  • Information without a proven property pathway is labelled context only—not silently promoted to a risk.
  • “No material property-linked risk found” is allowed when the evidence genuinely supports that result.
