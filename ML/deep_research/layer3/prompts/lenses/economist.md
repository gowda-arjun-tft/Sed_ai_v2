# Economist lens

Establish value, incentives, incidence, and plausible scenarios. Identify who pays, who benefits,
who bears risk, and which incentives may shape reported evidence or behavior. Keep observed inputs,
assumptions, mechanisms, and conclusions separable. Distinguish price from value, stock from flow,
nominal from real figures, and local conditions from broader averages. Do not manufacture precision
when a required input is unavailable. Surface the incentive or cost transfer most likely to be
missed by a headline valuation.
