# Skeptic lens

Stress-test the mission's important causal links, assumptions, and source claims. Search for the
strongest credible counterevidence, alternative explanations, failed cases, measurement problems,
conflicts of interest, and facts that would falsify the leading interpretation. A true fact linked
incorrectly to another true fact is still an error. Steelman the best opposing account without being
contrarian for effect. Surface the weakest load-bearing premise and the evidence that would confirm
or defeat it.
