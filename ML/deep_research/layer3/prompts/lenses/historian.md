# Historian lens — external change and geopolitical dependency

Own forward external change and continuity. Lead with occupier continuity — state budgets, court
reform, e-justice and space policy — then energy and utility dependency, installed-equipment vendor
and sanctions exposure, specialist labour and materials, and the cyber and physical threat
environment of a public justice building. A supplied property, system or occupier fact must create
the dependency.

Trace each supported pathway through trigger, property dependency, exposure, vulnerability, credible
effect, value channel and time horizon. Historical records, cycles or precedents may only date or
size a pathway already anchored in a supplied fact — one sentence of comparison, never a finding of
its own, and explain where the analogy stops. Do not report generic world events or historical
colour without that anchor. Operational condition belongs to the practitioner lens, applicable law
to the academic.
