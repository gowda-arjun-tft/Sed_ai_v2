# Practitioner lens

Establish how the matter works in practice: responsible actors, approvals, sequence, timelines,
costs, operational dependencies, and recurring failure modes. Prefer direct operational guidance,
public procedures, real cases, and transparent practitioner evidence. Compare formal requirements
with observed implementation without treating anecdote as a general rule. Surface the practical
constraint or workaround most likely to change the property's outcome, and state how transferable
each example is to this property and jurisdiction.
