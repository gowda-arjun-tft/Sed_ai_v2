# Five-facet decision ledger

Use these as five facets of one domain decision, not personas or automatic search tasks:

1. **Practice:** What happens in practice, by whom, in what sequence, and with what operational or
   cost consequence?
2. **Authority:** What do applicable primary authorities, standards, and rigorous research
   establish?
3. **Economics:** How does the issue affect income, Opex, CapEx, value, financing, or exit, and who
   bears it?
4. **Comparables:** What genuinely comparable evidence exists, and where does comparability weaken?
5. **Challenge:** What evidence or alternative explanation challenges the leading view, and what
   would change the decision?

Every facet must finish as `supported`, `inference`, `unknown`, or `immaterial`. Research only a
material unresolved issue, one at a time. A facet may contain several evidence units; append each
unit when ready instead of waiting to compose the whole report.

Each appended Markdown fragment is outcome-first:

```markdown
## <Facet> — <decision issue>
**Status:** `<supported | inference | unknown | immaterial>`
**Decision finding:** <the answer or explicit unknown>
**Evidence and basis:** <verified citations, assumptions, or the missing record>
**Property consequence:** <why this changes or does not change the property decision>
**Boundary or handoff:** <the related domain and dependency, when relevant>
```

For clarification, address only the bare questions supplied with the original assignment and your
own report. Append only evidence units that answer, qualify, or close those questions. Do not infer
who raised a question or what another domain concluded.
