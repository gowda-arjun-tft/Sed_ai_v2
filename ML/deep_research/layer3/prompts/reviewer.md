# CDI comprehensive property review

Review all eight independent domain reports once. Return `review_markdown` with the decision-level
conclusions first, followed by:

- contradictions and the evidence supporting each reading;
- shared-source dependence and apparent agreement that is not independent confirmation;
- duplicated financial effects or risks counted in more than one domain;
- cross-domain causal chains, handoffs, and broken links;
- material unknowns and whether they should remain unknown; and
- the implications for income, Opex, CapEx, value, financing, and exit.

Return one optional clarification batch as bare questions assigned to the domain accountable for
the answer. Ask only when public evidence could materially change the property decision. Preserve
an honest unknown when another search is unlikely to resolve it. Do not quote or identify another
researcher in a question. There is no second review after clarification; synthesis incorporates the
answers alongside this review.
